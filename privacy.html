The short version

Undigital collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The App Store's privacy label for this app reads "Data Not Collected".

Your footage is processed on your Mac, inside Final Cut Pro, and never leaves it.

Your footage

Undigital is an effect that Final Cut Pro runs on the frames of the clips you apply it to. Final Cut hands each frame to the effect, the effect renders the film look on your Mac's graphics processor and hands the frame back. Frames are not saved by Undigital, not copied anywhere and not sent anywhere.

What Undigital stores on your Mac

  • The effects themselves, installed as Motion templates in Movies ▸ Motion Templates, where Final Cut Pro looks for effects.
  • Your settings, kept with the standard macOS preferences system. Looks you save as Effects Presets are saved by Final Cut Pro itself, in your Final Cut library or presets, like any other effect's settings.
  • The optional film data, if you download it: public film datasheet files, kept in a container on your Mac shared only by the Undigital app and its effect.
  • Proof of an Undigital Pro purchase, if you buy it: the signed receipt for that one purchase, which the App Store gives the app, kept in the same container so the effect knows not to draw the watermark. It names the app, the purchase and its dates, not you.

Nothing is synced or uploaded by Undigital. Deleting the app and the Undigital folder in Movies ▸ Motion Templates removes it.

The one network request, and only if you ask

The Undigital window offers an optional download of measured film stocks. Only if you click that button does the app request public data files from GitHub (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com). The request carries nothing about you beyond what any web request carries: your IP address, which GitHub sees. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that request.

The Final Cut Pro effect itself never connects to the internet.

Buying Undigital Pro

Undigital is free; Undigital Pro, a one-off in-app purchase, removes the watermark. Buying it, restoring it and checking it go through Apple's App Store, with your Apple Account, as for any app. The developer gets no payment details, name or email from it. Apple's own privacy policy covers your App Store account and purchases.

The permissions it holds, and why

Undigital and its effect run in Apple's App Sandbox, which allows an app only the capabilities it asks for:

App Sandbox

Required for every Mac App Store app, and on for both the app and the effect.

Movies folder (app only)

To install the effects in Movies ▸ Motion Templates, where Final Cut Pro finds them. Undigital does not read your films or anything else in that folder.

Outgoing network connections (app only)

For the optional film data download described above. Purchases go through Apple's App Store, which the app reaches through macOS itself.

App group

A private folder shared by the Undigital app and its effect, so the effect can use the film data the app downloaded and see whether Undigital Pro was bought.

Analytics

None. Undigital contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If you have chosen to share analytics with Apple in System Settings, Apple may receive crash reports for any app, including this one; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer sees only what Apple chooses to pass on, without identifying you.

Children

Undigital collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because Undigital collects and holds no personal data, there is nothing about you to access, correct, export or delete. If you email for support, your email is used only to reply to you and is not shared.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does with data ever changes, this page will change first, with the date below updated.
