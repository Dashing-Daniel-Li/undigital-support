Undigital

Film emulation for Final Cut Pro.

Privacy Policy

What it does

Undigital is an effect for Final Cut Pro that gives your footage the look of motion picture and still film. It runs each frame through a model of the whole photochemical chain (the camera film, its development, the print and the projector) and then adds what film itself adds: halation, grain, gate weave, dust and scratches.

The Undigital app is the effect's installer and guide. Open it once and it installs the effects into Final Cut Pro; after that, everything happens in Final Cut.

Getting started

  1. Open Undigital once. It shows "The Undigital effects are installed in Final Cut Pro".
  2. Quit and reopen Final Cut Pro. Final Cut looks for new effects when it starts.
  3. Open the Effects browser (click the Effects button at the top right of the timeline, or press ⌘5) and choose the Undigital category.
  4. Skim over a look to preview it on your clip, then drag it onto the clip.
  5. Fine-tune it in the inspector: Film Look, Amount, Exposure, Warmth, Contrast, Grain and Halation are at the top. The finer controls are in the sections underneath.

Common questions

I installed Undigital but the effects are not in Final Cut Pro.

Open the Undigital app once, so it can install the effects, then quit Final Cut Pro completely (⌘Q) and open it again. Final Cut only looks for new effects when it starts. In the Effects browser, choose the Undigital category, or type "Undigital" in the browser's search field.

If the Undigital window says the effects could not be installed, check that your Movies folder can be written to: the effects are installed in Movies ▸ Motion Templates. Then open Undigital again.

How do I remove the watermark?

Undigital is free to use, and every look and control works, with an Undigital watermark on the picture. Undigital Pro, one purchase with no subscription, removes it. Open the Undigital app and click Remove Watermark.

If Final Cut Pro was open when you bought it, quit and reopen it. Clips Final Cut had already rendered keep the watermark until their render files are deleted: select the library, choose File ▸ Delete Generated Library Files, then Delete Render Files.

I bought Undigital Pro on another Mac, or reinstalled it.

Open the Undigital app on this Mac, signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, and click Restore Purchases. Then restart Final Cut Pro.

The look is too strong, or too subtle.

Amount, the second row of the inspector, sets how much of the whole effect you see, from 0% (your original picture) to 100%. For a stronger texture, raise Grain and Halation; for a different colour, try another Film Look or add a Colour Style on top. To compare with the original, untick the Undigital checkbox in the inspector.

Faces look orange or grey.

Set Exposure first so faces sit where you want them: film is kind to a slightly bright clip and harsh to a dark one. If skin still looks wrong, ease off Colour Separation (in Camera Film) or lower Style Amount.

How do I use it with log footage?

If Final Cut Pro is already converting your log footage (its log conversion is on in the clip's settings), leave Camera on Automatic. If you have switched Final Cut's conversion off, open Source Footage in the inspector and choose your camera's log under Camera.

How do I apply the same look to a whole scene?

Copy a graded clip (⌘C), select the other clips, then choose Edit ▸ Paste Attributes and tick Undigital. To keep a look for later, click Save Effects Preset at the bottom of the inspector; it appears in the Effects browser.

What are the measured film stocks?

An optional, free download offered in the Undigital window: more film stocks and prints built from the film makers' published datasheets, about 6 MB. The data is by Andrea Volpato (the spektrafilm project), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The download only happens when you click the button.

Are the looks exact copies of real films?

No. The built-in stocks are models of how each kind of film behaves, and the film looks in the Film Look menu are matched to well-known films, not measured from the films themselves. Film names in the app say which product a look is modelled on or matched to. Undigital is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, their makers.

Why does Undigital ask for access to my Movies folder?

Final Cut Pro finds effects in Movies ▸ Motion Templates, so that is where Undigital installs them. It does not read your films or anything else in that folder.

Does it work in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro?

Not yet. Undigital works in Final Cut Pro. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
